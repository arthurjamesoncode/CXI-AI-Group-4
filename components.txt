Design System Components

Component: Section Heading
Purpose: Label a content section clearly and establish hierarchy.
Best used in: Hero Section, Brand Story Section, Featured Items Section, Menu Listing Section, Enquiry Form Section, Location & Opening Hours Section, CTA Banner Section.
Content guidance: Keep headings short, memorable, and easy to scan. Prefer plain English over marketing language.
Avoid: Long sentence-style headings or abstract wording.

Component: Supporting Paragraph
Purpose: Add short explanatory copy below a heading or title.
Best used in: Hero Section, Brand Story Section, Featured Items Section, Enquiry Form Section, CTA Banner Section.
Content guidance: Use warm, human, concise language. Usually one to three sentences maximum.
Avoid: Corporate jargon, overly polished sales language, or dense copy blocks.

Component: Eyebrow Label
Purpose: Add a small contextual label above a heading to orient the user.
Best used in: Hero Section, Featured Items Section, CTA Banner Section.
Content guidance: Examples include “This week”, “Freshly baked”, or “Made to order”.
Avoid: Overusing on every section.

Component: Primary Button
Purpose: Drive the most important action in a section.
Best used in: Hero Section, CTA Banner Section, Enquiry Form Section.
Content guidance: Use clear action-led labels such as “View Menu”, “Order a Cake”, or “Send Enquiry”.
Avoid: Using multiple primary buttons in a small section unless there is a strong reason.

Component: Secondary Link
Purpose: Offer a lower-emphasis navigation or supporting action.
Best used in: Hero Section, Brand Story Section, Footer Section.
Content guidance: Keep labels short and direct.
Avoid: Competing visually with the primary action.

Component: Image Block
Purpose: Add atmosphere, product visibility, or brand warmth through photography.
Best used in: Hero Section, Brand Story Section, Featured Items Section, Image Gallery Section.
Content guidance: Prefer authentic bakery, food, or local-environment imagery.
Avoid: Generic corporate stock photography or overly polished lifestyle imagery.

Component: Section Image Pair
Purpose: Combine text and image in a balanced two-column layout.
Best used in: Brand Story Section, Featured Items Section.
Content guidance: Use when a section benefits from both explanation and visual support.
Avoid: Overusing repeatedly on every section.

Component: Product Card
Purpose: Present an individual menu item or featured product clearly.
Best used in: Featured Items Section, Menu Listing Section.
Content guidance: Include item name, short description if useful, and price where relevant.
Avoid: Long descriptions that reduce scanability.

Component: Product Row
Purpose: Display menu items in a more compact line-based format.
Best used in: Menu Listing Section.
Content guidance: Suitable for simple menus with item name, short descriptor, and price.
Avoid: Over-styling or making the layout too dense.

Component: Category Group
Purpose: Organise related menu items under a shared heading.
Best used in: Menu Listing Section.
Content guidance: Good examples include Bread, Pastries, Cakes, Specials.
Avoid: Too many categories with very few items each.

Component: Price Text
Purpose: Show item pricing clearly and consistently.
Best used in: Product Card, Product Row, Menu Listing Section.
Content guidance: Place close to the item name and keep formatting consistent.
Avoid: Hiding prices or making them visually secondary if price matters to users.

Component: Badge / Tag
Purpose: Highlight a useful status or special note on an item.
Best used in: Featured Items Section, Menu Listing Section.
Content guidance: Good examples include “Seasonal”, “Popular”, “This week”, or “Made to order”.
Avoid: Decorative labels with no real meaning.

Component: Divider
Purpose: Separate groups of content without heavy visual interruption.
Best used in: Menu Listing Section, long-form informational pages, Footer Section.
Content guidance: Keep visual treatment subtle.
Avoid: Replacing proper spacing or hierarchy with too many lines.

Component: Form Intro Text
Purpose: Explain the purpose of a form and set expectations before input begins.
Best used in: Enquiry Form Section.
Content guidance: Make the process feel simple and low-pressure.
Avoid: Legalistic or overly formal instructions.

Component: Form Field
Purpose: Collect a single structured piece of information from the user.
Best used in: Enquiry Form Section.
Content guidance: Use explicit labels such as Name, Date Needed, Email, or Phone.
Avoid: Ambiguous labels.

Component: Textarea
Purpose: Let users describe a custom request in their own words.
Best used in: Enquiry Form Section.
Content guidance: Encourage users to share details such as flavour, size, occasion, or decoration ideas.
Avoid: Making it feel compulsory to write too much.

Component: Submit Button
Purpose: Send form content for review or enquiry.
Best used in: Enquiry Form Section.
Content guidance: Labels should be reassuring and direct, such as “Send Enquiry”.
Avoid: Aggressive or overly sales-focused language.

Component: Contact Detail Row
Purpose: Display one practical contact item clearly.
Best used in: Contact Details Section, Location & Opening Hours Section, Footer Section.
Content guidance: Suitable for phone number, email, address, or enquiry contact.
Avoid: Decorative clutter around essential details.

Component: Opening Hours List
Purpose: Present weekly opening times in a scannable structure.
Best used in: Location & Opening Hours Section.
Content guidance: Use clear day/time pairing and make exceptions easy to notice.
Avoid: Dense paragraph formatting.

Component: Map Embed Placeholder
Purpose: Represent a location map within a page layout.
Best used in: Location & Opening Hours Section.
Content guidance: The map should support practical information rather than dominate the page.
Avoid: Oversized map elements that overwhelm the section.

Component: Social Link
Purpose: Link to an external social channel where relevant.
Best used in: Footer Section, Contact Details Section.
Content guidance: Use only if the channel adds value and feels current.
Avoid: Making social links more prominent than the primary business actions.

Component: Small Print
Purpose: Hold lightweight utility text such as copyright or policy references.
Best used in: Footer Section.
Content guidance: Keep concise and visually quiet.
Avoid: Letting this compete with core navigation or contact details.

Component: Navigation Link Group
Purpose: Offer a compact set of page links for easier site movement.
Best used in: Header areas, Footer Section.
Content guidance: Keep link labels familiar and minimal, such as Home, Menu, Order a Cake, Find Us.
Avoid: Large or complex navigation systems for a very small site.
