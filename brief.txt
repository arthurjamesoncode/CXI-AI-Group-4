A local bakery — "Harwood & Sons"
A family-run bakery in the Peak District wants a simple website to replace their outdated Facebook page as their main online presence. They do sourdough, pastries, and seasonal celebration cakes, and take custom cake orders by request.
Goals

Show their menu and weekly specials
Let customers find opening hours, location, and contact details
Accept custom cake enquiries via a contact form

Pages

Home — hero image, short brand story, link to menu
Menu — bread, pastries, and cakes with prices
Order a cake — simple enquiry form (name, date needed, message)
Find us — map embed, opening hours, phone number

Tone
Warm, unpretentious, artisan. Nothing corporate. Feels like it was made with the same care as the bread.
