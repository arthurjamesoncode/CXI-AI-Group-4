Upload your Design System

This page is meant to generate a new webpage based on a brief (or briefs) and a given design system which you can upload using the boxes below.

Each section collects a specific part of your design system. Your system may or may not fit the categories included. Add, rename, or remove sections to match your needs.

Once finished, hit submit and wait. Your chosen AI agent will use the uploaded documents to select which elements of your system it thinks should be used.

Under each selection, you can see a justification of why it chose those specific elements. At the bottom of a page is a html file, which contains a mock up page made of the elements it has chosen.

Disclaimer: This page sends requests to various cloud AI services, you should not upload any sensitive information as part of your design system.
